Guide & Strategy for Tech Internships & Entry-Level Roles

Key strategies for landing PFE internships and junior developer / AI / cybersecurity positions.

1. Direct Cold Email / LinkedIn Outreach

Don't just apply through job buttons! Search for the Engineering Lead, Tech Recruiter, or CTO on LinkedIn. Use the "Cold Email" generator button on JobRadar to send them a concise, direct message referencing their specific open role.

2. Portfolio & Proof of Work

For AI, Cyber, or Dev roles: 2 deployed, high-quality projects with clean READMEs, live demo links, and Docker files beat 20 generic classroom exercises. Link them directly in your profile.

3. For Cybersecurity Roles

Highlight hands-on labs: TryHackMe / HackTheBox rank, write-ups on Medium/GitHub, certifications (e.g. eJPT, Security+, CEH), and practical experience with SIEM (Splunk, Wazuh) or network forensics (Wireshark).

4. For AI & Data Engineer Roles

Focus on end-to-end ML pipelines: data ingestion, vector databases (RAG / Pinecone / Chroma), API deployment (FastAPI / Docker), and fine-tuning rather than just running Jupyter notebooks.
